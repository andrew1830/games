Tri-Bond

TriBond

How to play

Goal

Each card is a Threezer: three clues with something in common. Name the bond.

A round

  • 10 cards. You get three guesses per card.
  • Points: 3 for a first-guess solve, 2 for the second, 1 for the third. Top score is 30.
  • Hint shows the shape of the answer (first letters) and costs a point, but a solve is always worth at least 1.
  • Give up to see the answer and move on.

Answering

  • Say it your way: "black and white", "they're all black & white" and "black white" all count.
  • If you were right but it wasn't accepted, tap Count it after the answer shows.

Categories

Entertainment, Academics, Sports, Wordplay and Loose Connections. Wordplay bonds are often a hidden word: "Butter, Dragon, Fire" → words before fly. Pick one category or a mix in Settings.
