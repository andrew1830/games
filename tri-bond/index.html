How to play

Goal

Be the first to move all three of your pawns around their paths and into the Home spaces. You play red; the computer plays blue.

A turn

  • Roll the category die and the number die.
  • You get a Threezer from that category: three clues that share a bond. Type the bond and tap Check, or tap Reveal and grade yourself.
  • Right answer: move one pawn forward by the number rolled. A roll can't be split between pawns.
  • Wrong answer: you don't move. Play passes.
  • Free roll (★): no riddle — just move.

Moving

  • Each pawn stays on its own path and moves clockwise: 17 spaces, then Home.
  • Pawns can't share a space. Land on an occupied space and you move on to the next open one.
  • You don't need an exact roll to get Home.

Categories

Entertainment, Academics, Sports, Wordplay and Loose Connections. Wordplay bonds are often a hidden word: "Butter, Dragon, Fire" → words before fly.

Two-player game

As in the official two-player rules, Challenges and stealing are left out. Short game uses one path instead of three.
