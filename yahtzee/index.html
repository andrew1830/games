Yahtzee

Space roll · 1–5 hold · Enter score · H hint
Stats
How to play
  • 13 rounds. Each turn you get up to three rolls. Tap dice to hold them between rolls.
  • After any roll you can score: tap a box, then press Score. Every turn fills exactly one box, so sometimes you have to take a 0.
  • Upper section: add up only the dice showing that number. Reach 63 or more (three of each) for a 35-point bonus. The small +/− next to your subtotal shows how far ahead or behind that pace you are.
  • 3 / 4 of a Kind: total of all five dice. Full House (three of one, two of another): 25. Small Straight (four in a row): 30. Large Straight (five in a row): 40. Yahtzee (five alike): 50. Chance: total of all dice.
  • Yahtzee bonus: every extra Yahtzee after scoring 50 in the Yahtzee box earns 100 more.
  • Joker rules for any extra Yahtzee: you must fill the matching upper box if it's open. If not, use any open lower box, where Full House and the straights score their full 25 / 30 / 40. If those are full too, take a 0 in an upper box. A 0 in the Yahtzee box means no bonus, but the Joker rules still apply.
  • Five of a kind counts as a Full House only under the Joker rules.
  • Against the computer, the higher total after 13 rounds wins. Hint uses the expert computer's strategy; Undo (solo only) takes back your last box until you roll again.
