Connect Four

Stats
How to play
  • You're red, the computer is yellow. Take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns; it falls to the lowest open space.
  • Four of your discs in a row, across, up and down, or diagonally, wins.
  • If all 42 spaces fill with no four in a row, it's a draw.
  • Who goes first alternates from game to game. Moving first is an advantage.
  • Hint shows the move the Hard computer would make for you. Undo takes back your last move and the computer's reply. Games where you use either aren't counted in your stats.
  • On a keyboard, press 1–7 to drop a disc.
