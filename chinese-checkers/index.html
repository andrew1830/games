    How to play
    • Race all 10 of your red marbles from the bottom point of the star into the point straight across.
    • On your turn, move one marble: either step to an empty neighboring hole, or jump over a single adjacent marble of any color into the empty hole just beyond. Jumps can chain in any direction, as far as you like. You can't mix a step with jumps.
    • Tap a marble to see where it can go, then tap a highlighted hole. The marble follows the jump path.
    • You can pass through other points of the star, but you can't stop in a point that isn't your home or your target.
    • Once a marble reaches your target point it stays there, though it can move around inside it.
    • The first player to fill their target point wins. If an opponent's marbles never left that point, they count toward filling it, so leaving them there can't block you.
    • Undo takes back your last move and the computers' replies. Hint shows the move the Hard computer would make.
