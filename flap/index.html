Flap

Tap to start

Rules

Goal

Keep the bat in the air and fly through as many gaps as you can.

Controls

  • Tap the game, press the Flap button, or hit Space or ↑ to flap.
  • Between flaps the bat falls.

Scoring

1 point for each pillar pair you clear.

Game over

Touching a pillar or the ground ends the run. The ceiling is safe. The game pauses on its own if you switch tabs or open a sheet.
