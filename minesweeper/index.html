How to play
  • Open every square that isn't a mine. A number tells you how many of the eight squares around it hold mines.
  • Your first tap is always safe and opens an area.
  • Flag a mine with a long-press or right-click, or switch the ⛏️/🚩 toggle to flag mode, where a tap flags and a long-press opens.
  • Chord: tap a number whose flags are all placed to open the rest of its neighbors. If one of those flags is wrong, you'll set off a mine.
  • Beginner is 9×9 with 10 mines, Intermediate 16×16 with 40, Expert 30×16 with 99 (shown tall on phones).
  • No-guess boards are built so pure logic can clear them. Classic boards sometimes end in a true coin flip.
  • Hint finds a square that's certainly safe, or points out a wrong flag. When nothing is certain, it shows the least risky square and its odds. Tap Hint again to make that move.
  • Undo takes back your last move, even a mine. Games with a hint or undo still count as wins, but not toward best times.
  • Tap the face to start over.
