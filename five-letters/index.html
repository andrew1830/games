Gray: the letter is not in the word, or not again. A repeated letter turns gray once you've used up its copies.

  • Play as many words as you like. Press Next word when a round ends.
  • Hint shows one correct letter in its spot. Wins with hints don't count toward your streak.
  • New word mid-round gives up the round and counts as a loss.
  • Hard mode: every green and gold letter you find must be used in later guesses.
