Rules

Goal

Score as high as you can in 30 moves.

A move

Swap two neighboring pieces (tap one, then the other, or swipe). The swap must make a line of 3 or more of the same shape. Otherwise it slides back and costs nothing.

Clearing

Matched pieces clear, pieces above fall, and new ones drop in. Chains score more: each cascade multiplies the points per piece.

Special pieces

  • Line of 4 makes a striped piece. When cleared it wipes its whole row or column (the stripes show which).
  • L or T shape makes a bomb. When cleared it clears the 3×3 area around it.
  • Line of 5 makes a color burst. Swap it with any piece to clear every piece of that shape.
  • Swap two specials together to combine them. Two bursts clear the whole board.

No moves left

If no swap is possible, the board reshuffles.
