    How to play

    This is Kalah, the standard Mancala rule set. Your six pits are the bottom row (on a phone, the left column) and your store is on the right (bottom on a phone). The computer owns the other side.

    • On your turn, pick up every seed in one of your pits and sow them one at a time, counter-clockwise, into the following pits — including your own store, but skipping the computer's store.
    • Extra turn: if your last seed lands in your store, go again.
    • Capture: if your last seed lands in an empty pit on your side and the pit directly across has seeds, take both the last seed and everything across into your store.
    • End: as soon as either side's six pits are all empty, the game ends. Each player adds the seeds left on their own side to their store. Most seeds wins.

    Undo takes back your last move and the computer's reply. Hint asks the Hard engine for your best move.
