    Rules & trainer

    You play the light checkers, moving counter-clockwise from the top right toward your home board at bottom right. Bring all 15 checkers home, then bear them off. First to bear off all 15 wins.

    • Moving: each die is a separate move. Doubles play four times. You must use both dice if you can; if only one can be played, you must play the larger when possible.
    • Hitting: landing on a single enemy checker (a blot) sends it to the bar. A checker on the bar must re-enter in the opponent's home board before anything else moves. Two or more checkers make a point that can't be landed on.
    • Bearing off: once all your checkers are home, remove them by exact roll, or with a higher roll from your highest point.
    • Scoring: single game 1, gammon 2 (loser bore off none), backgammon 3 (loser also has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board), times the cube.
    • Doubling cube: before rolling you may double. The opponent takes (plays on for twice the stakes and owns the cube) or passes (loses the current stake). Money-game rules: no Jacoby rule, no beavers.

    How to move: tap a checker to see where it can go (gold), then tap a gold point. Tapping a point only one checker can reach moves it straight there. Tap the tray to bear off.

    Trainer: every move and cube decision is checked by a neural-net evaluator (one-ply lookahead). Errors are measured in equity — points per game at stake 1. Under 0.04 is fine, 0.04–0.08 an inaccuracy, 0.08–0.16 a mistake, 0.16+ a blunder. Hint lists the top plays. The evaluator is strong but not perfect; treat close calls (within ~0.02) as ties.
