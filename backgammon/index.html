Moves

    Rules

    Goal

    Move all 15 of your light checkers into your home board (bottom right), then bear them off. First to bear off all 15 wins.

    A turn

    • You move counter-clockwise, from the top right toward point 1. The CPU moves the other way.
    • Each die is a separate move. Doubles are played four times.
    • Use both dice if you can. If only one can be played, play the larger when possible.
    • You can't land on a point held by two or more enemy checkers.

    Hitting and the bar

    • Landing on a lone enemy checker (a blot) sends it to the bar.
    • A checker on the bar must re-enter in the opponent's home board before anything else moves.

    Bearing off

    Once all your checkers are home, remove them with an exact roll, or with a higher roll from your highest point.

    Scoring and the cube

    • A win scores 1, a gammon 2 (loser bore off none), a backgammon 3 (loser also has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board) — times the cube.
    • Before rolling you may double. The other side takes (plays on at twice the stakes and owns the cube) or passes (loses the current stake).
    • Money-game rules: no Jacoby rule, no beavers.

    Controls

    • Tap a checker to see where it can go, then tap a highlighted spot. Or drag it there.
    • Tap a spot only one checker can reach to move straight there. Tap the tray to bear off.
    • Keyboard: Enter for the main button, U to undo, H for a hint.

    Coach

    With the coach on, every move and cube decision is checked by a neural-net evaluator with one roll of lookahead. Errors are in equity (points per game at stake 1): under 0.04 is fine, 0.04–0.08 an inaccuracy, 0.08–0.16 a mistake, 0.16 or more a blunder.
